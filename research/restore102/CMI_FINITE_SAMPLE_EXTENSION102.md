# Post-initial-run finite-sample CMI extension
Initial triple metrics were computed before this extension. For each root and fixed triple of clocks, condition on observed AB and BC margins. Under random independent matching of earlier and later states within each middle state, N_abc has a hypergeometric marginal, mean N_ab N_bc/N_b. Compute exact expected empirical plug-in CMI, sum over all cells E[N log(N/E[N])]/N. No shuffling of clocks or roots. This is a finite conditional-permutation baseline, not an unbiased estimator or a p-value; subtract baseline descriptively. Compare independent enumeration on an exact tiny table. Existing train/heldout models and admission criteria unchanged. Treat absent/zero-variance normalized ACF as undefined, retain mask and report valid-root counts, do not aggregate zero sentinel as measured rho.
