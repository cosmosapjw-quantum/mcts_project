## Full-history construction (derived)
Fix root geometry, evaluator weights, intervention, budget B=128, wave width 4; let H_t denote the complete pre-wave search history and internal phase state. Transition K_t(h,dh',dy,dz) is normalized on actual ordered search/backup events (including leaf-value sign, action and completion order). Y_t=(N_a,N_b,N_rest), Z_t=sum root-frame backup values (dimensionless). X=sum_t (Y_a,Y_b,Z_t)/B. Rest count is fixed by total; do not invert a redundant 3-count Hessian. Grouped count conditional law under R65 phase instrument is the mixture d Mult(4,p0)+(1-d) Mult(4,p1) where d=(1+v_y sin(.7)+v_z cos(.7))/2, p_i=(m e_i,m(1-e_i),1-m), e0=.35r+.65,e1=.35r. This follows from commuting effects, conditional on actual pre-wave state; Rapfi clean root law is frozen-within-wave multinomial. Mark law depends on full continuation and is not determined by count marginal.

Define F_T(h)=1, F_t(h)=integral K_t(h,dh',dy,dz) exp[J·(y_a,y_b,z)/B] F_{t+1}(h'). Then W(J)=log integral nu(dh0) F_0(h0)=log E exp(J·X). This exact finite Feynman-Kac recursion follows by backward conditional expectation, no independence of adaptive waves. In general W is neither sum of unconditional local W_t nor E sum log conditional Z_t. The count-only product L_T=prod exp[j·Y_t]/E[exp(j·Y_t)|H_t] is a mean-one martingale: conditional expectation of L_{t+1} given H_t equals L_t; not an estimate of exp(W). Explicit frozen, adaptive conditional and history-reduced teacher-forced predictions are compared, not conflated with autonomous forward propagation.

For any fixed positive finite measure (in particular empirical registered complete histories), W(0)=0, W'=E_J X, W''=Cov_J X. Directional third and fourth derivatives are centered third moment and centered fourth minus 3 variance squared. Affine-null directions of support removed; on affinely spanning support W'' positive definite at finite J. Gamma(x)=sup_J(J·x-W(J)) on convex support. For interior image x=W'(J), Gamma'=J, Gamma''=(W'')^{-1}; exterior has infinite conjugate; as J concentrates on exposed face, covariance degeneracy and small ESS may prevent population inference. This statistical 1PI analogue has no physical hbar; empirical positivity only on observed support. Three coordinates include mark; no claim field-functional limit.

## Independent replicas and controlled saddle (derived)
Let A be integer full-history source-a count, law p supported on finite lattice with maximal span h. For M independent replicas of the *whole* adaptive history (independent root draw if using empirical mixture), let k/M be interior, K(s)=log E exp(sA), K'(s)=k/M, k2=K'', k3=K''', k4=K''''. On attainable lattice points with span one, fixed interior compact source regime and k2>0, tilting and Fourier inversion give P(S_M=k)=exp[M K(s)-sk] times integral of centered tilted characteristic function to power M divided by 2pi over one reciprocal period. Expand log phi around u=0, put u=z/sqrt(M k2). Odd term integrates to zero; Gaussian z^4 and z^6 moments are 3 and 15. Thus
P(S_M=k) ~ exp[-M Gamma(k/M)] / sqrt(2 pi M k2) * [1+(k4/(8 k2^2)-5 k3^2/(24 k2^3))/M+O(M^-2)].
For general maximal span h multiply by h on support; no result for unreachable lattice sites. Remainder for fixed finite law and compact s follows from analyticity and exponential decay away from the origin (span one), Taylor expansion and Gaussian domination; no uniform bound at boundary or sparse empirical support. Negative log probability = M Gamma + .5 log(2pi M k2) - log(1+c/M+...). The replica limit M is not B or inverse hbar. This is a local lattice Laplace expansion, not the original real-time quantum loop. Wolfram Gaussian integral returned (3a-5b^2)/24 for standardized cumulants. Primary literature context: Howard et al. 2021 https://doi.org/10.1214/20-AOS1991 (conditional exponential concentration); Buhler et al. https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.08845 (bounded lattice Edgeworth errors; not the exact local formula used here); Skovgaard 1987 https://scispace.com/papers/saddlepoint-expansions-for-conditional-distributions-1hnrwdlz7r (conditional lattice saddlepoint context). The explicit coefficient above is directly derived and checked, not attributed to these excerpts as theorem.
