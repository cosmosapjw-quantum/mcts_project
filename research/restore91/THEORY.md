Native occupancy E_score=-Σ_i (n_i/B)(w_i/n_i)=-Σ_{k<=B} z_k/B, z_k=(-1)^depth leaf root frame. A proxy B^-1/2 is not an independently calibrated temperature. For a fixed pre-wave score s_i, log p_i=beta_p s_i-log Z gives beta_p only for nonconstant s. Endpoints are history-dependent occupancy, and minimizing D(occ||softmax(b Q)) yields moment matching in interior: gradient E_b[Q]-E_occ[Q], curvature Var_b(Q). Flat/near-flat objectives imply weak parameter identification. Use probability and KL identity, not equality of poorly conditioned fitted beta.
For fixed H, Gibbs p(theta) and U=Σpe, dU/dtheta=Var(e)/theta^2=C/k_B; e fixed, theta dimensionless. Pairwise variance identity Var(e)=1/2 Σ_ij p_i p_j(e_i-e_j)^2. No native physical T.
Feedback: p Gibbs for e0; measurement M_im and pm=Σ_i p_iM_im. a_m=p_i M_im/pm; pi_m Gibbs for e_m; b_m=(1-alpha)a_m+alpha pi_m. Q_m=Σ_i(b_m-a_m)e_m; W_m=Σ_i(a_m-b_m)(e_m-e0); r=Σ_m pm b_m. Delta U=W+Q. Since pi_m strictly positive, convexity gives D(b_m||pi_m)<=(1-alpha)D(a_m||pi_m); Σ_rel=Σ pm[D(a_m||pi_m)-D(b_m||pi_m)]>=0. Using log pi_m=-beta e_m-log Z_m gives Σ_rel=Delta H_cond-beta Q. Chain rule Delta H_cond=H(r)-H(p)-I1+I0. Finally D(r||p)=beta Delta U-[H(r)-H(p)]. Substitution yields beta W+I0=Σ_rel+D(r||p)+I1. This is exact at fixed finite positive beta, normalized finite channels and declared detailed-balanced refresh kernel. It is not an inferred native reservoir.
Forward and reverse: F_ijm=p_i M_im P_m(i,j); R_ijm=pm p_j P_m(j,i); log F/R=beta W+log(M_im/pm) on F>0; sum F exp(-log F/R)=R(supp F)=1-lambda. The Gibbs start and same Hamiltonian endpoint imply Delta F=0 but not r=p; no cyclic engine efficiency or controller reset cost implied.
At repeated fixed conditional relaxation, p_m fixed and b_m(t)=.75^t a_m+(1-.75^t)pi_m. Signed entropy balance sigma_t=Delta H(r_t)-Delta I_t-beta Q_t. Exact model evolution, not MCTS physical time. I0=0 at eps=1, but I1 generically positive because the conditional kernel depends on m. At alpha=0 W=Q=Sigma_rel=0, I1=I0; path log ratio may remain nonzero.
