# Supplemental original L08/L32 information-to-work construction
Freeze before generating feedback samples, after native and initial thermal/erasure data.
For each of the 96 pilot source pairs at knot 4 use p(x) conditional on the pair (same p as erasure). Define binary detector P(m=x|x)=0.8 and P(m!=x|x)=0.2. Initial Hamiltonian E0(x)=-log p(x)/beta, Gibbs p. Measurement classical, ideal readout cost not included; record preparation/reset costs separate. For fixed m, conditional p_m is Bayes posterior; quench E0 to Em=-log p_m/beta (no state change), W0=-i(x,m)/beta where i=log p_m(x)/p(x). Then anneal Em to E0 through K=1,4,16,64 log-odds interpolation and full refresh after each quench. All normalized Gibbs Z=1. Mean total W = (-I+sum_m p(m) sum_j KL(pi_{m,j-1}||pi_{m,j}))/beta. The exact forward-reverse experiment samples m from p(m), independently in each direction. F initial conditional p_m, R starts Gibbs p, reverse heat-before-quench steps followed by reversed initial quench. On forward-oriented sequences, log(PF/PRrev)=beta W+i. E_F exp(-beta W-i)=1. Reverse support positive. Feedback efficiency -beta E W/I can be negative, <=1. Not a full device cycle (measurement/erasure/feedback memory cost omitted); not native physical work. 2048 samples each direction per 96 protocols x4 K, independent seeds 6800000+..., fixed accuracy .8.
