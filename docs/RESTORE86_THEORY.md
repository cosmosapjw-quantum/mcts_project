Exact nonstationary memory: use a fixed probability measure on full trajectories omega and measurable Y_i at clock 8(i+1), i=0,...,15. E_i maps functions to conditional means on positive-mass Y_i support, B_i maps state vectors f to f(Y_i). E_i B_i=I, P_i=B_i E_i is the L² conditional-expectation orthogonal projector, Q_i=I-P_i. The sigma fields of Y_i need not be nested; do not invoke tower property on them. G_ij=E_i B_j is a stochastic conditional endpoint matrix. Define K_ik=E_i Q_(i+1)...Q_k B_(k+1), with empty Q product K_ii=G_i,i+1. Insert I=P_(i+1)+Q_(i+1) into E_i B_j, then insert P_(i+2)+Q_(i+2) to the right of preceding Q, and continue. P_j B_j=B_j ends the sum. This yields G_ij=sum_(k=i)^(j-1) K_ik G_(k+1,j) exactly, G_jj=I, hence K_ik=G_i,k+1-sum_(l=i)^(k-1)K_il G_l+1,k+1. Signed rows of K_ik for k>i sum to zero because Q annihilates constants; K_ii stochastic. This is a clock-dependent projection/renewal identity for conditional endpoints in one common ensemble, not an autonomous full path generator or a physical GLE.
