## Concentration, collision bias and symmetry
m(X)=(n sum_i X_i^2-1)/(n-1) in [0,1]. With independent identical categorical draws from p, E sum X_i^2=1/B+(1-1/B)sum p_i^2. Consequently E m(X)=1/B+(1-1/B)m(p). The corrected m*=(B m(X)-1)/(B-1) equals n/(n-1) times [sum_{s != t} 1(a_s=a_t)/(B(B-1))-1/n]; E m*=m(p) for iid.
